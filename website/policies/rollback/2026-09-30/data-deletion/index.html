Data Deletion

  • Effective Date: June 19, 2026
  • Website: andmorethings.com/data-deletion
  • Contact: privacy@andmorethings.com

SecurityForAll users may request deletion of personal data associated with their app usage.

1. How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion, email us at:

privacy@andmorethings.com

Please include:

  • The email address associated with your account, if applicable
  • Your approximate date of app usage
  • A short description of the data you want deleted
  • Any information needed to help us locate your request

We may ask for additional information to verify your request.

2. Account Deletion

If SecurityForAll includes account functionality, you may request deletion of your account and associated data through the app or by contacting us at:

privacy@andmorethings.com

If an in-app account deletion option is available, you can find it at:

Settings → Account → Delete Account

If your app version does not include account functionality, you may still request deletion of data associated with your email address, support request, purchase entitlement, app instance, or submitted content where we are able to locate it.

3. What May Be Deleted

Depending on your use of the app, deletion may include:

  • Account information
  • Support communications
  • User-submitted content associated with your account or request
  • Analysis history associated with your account or request
  • Saved scam reports associated with your account or request
  • App usage records associated with your account or request, where reasonably identifiable

4. What May Be Retained

Some information may be retained when necessary for:

  • Legal compliance
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Abuse prevention
  • Accounting and payment records
  • Subscription entitlement records
  • Dispute resolution
  • Platform compliance
  • Backup integrity
  • Legitimate operational needs

We may also retain aggregated, de-identified, or anonymized information that is not designed to identify individual users.

5. Subscriptions

Deleting your SecurityForAll account or data does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription.

To manage or cancel a subscription purchased through Google Play, use your Google Play account subscription settings.

6. Response Time

We will review and respond to deletion requests within a reasonable period of time and in accordance with applicable law.

7. Contact

For deletion requests or privacy questions, contact:

privacy@andmorethings.com
