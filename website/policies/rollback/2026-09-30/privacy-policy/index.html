SecurityForAll

Privacy Policy

  • Effective Date: June 19, 2026
  • Website: andmorethings.com
  • Contact: privacy@andmorethings.com

SecurityForAll helps users analyze suspicious messages, links, QR codes, screenshots, and similar content to identify possible scams, phishing attempts, fraud indicators, unsafe websites, impersonation attempts, or other security risks.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, retain, and protect information when you use SecurityForAll, our website, and related services.

SecurityForAll is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways.

Information You Choose to Submit

When you use SecurityForAll, you may choose to submit content for scam or security analysis. This may include:

  • Text messages or copied text
  • Suspicious URLs or website links
  • QR code contents
  • Screenshots or images you choose to upload
  • Descriptions of suspicious activity
  • Scam-related questions or reports
  • Other content you voluntarily provide for analysis

You should not submit passwords, payment card numbers, bank account numbers, government identification numbers, medical information, private account credentials, or other highly sensitive information.

Analysis Results and Security Indicators

When SecurityForAll analyzes submitted content, we may generate and store related information, such as:

  • Scam risk scores
  • Analysis results
  • Security explanations
  • Suspicious link indicators
  • QR code analysis results
  • Fraud category labels
  • Abuse-prevention signals
  • Aggregated or de-identified scam trend information

Device, App, and Usage Information

We may collect limited technical information needed to operate, secure, and improve the app, such as:

  • App version
  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App settings
  • General usage events
  • Diagnostic logs
  • Crash reports
  • Security tokens
  • App instance identifiers
  • Device-binding signals
  • Network request metadata, such as timestamps and basic service logs

We use device-binding signals to help prevent abuse, enforce usage limits, protect subscriptions or paid features, detect fraud, and maintain platform security.

Purchase and Subscription Information

If you purchase a subscription or premium feature, we may process information related to your purchase, such as:

  • Subscription status
  • Purchase entitlement status
  • Google Play purchase identifiers
  • Transaction-related metadata
  • Account or app instance identifiers associated with premium access

We do not collect or store your full payment card number. Payments and subscriptions are processed by Google Play or another authorized payment provider.

Support Communications

If you contact us for support, privacy questions, or data deletion requests, we may collect:

  • Your email address
  • The contents of your message
  • Information needed to respond to your request
  • Support history related to your request

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide scam, phishing, fraud, and security-risk analysis
  • Generate risk scores, explanations, and security recommendations
  • Analyze QR codes, URLs, screenshots, and user-submitted text
  • Improve detection quality and app reliability
  • Create aggregated or de-identified scam trend reports
  • Prevent abuse of the platform
  • Enforce usage limits
  • Protect paid features and subscriptions
  • Detect fraud, misuse, or unauthorized access
  • Provide customer support
  • Diagnose crashes and technical issues
  • Maintain the security and integrity of our services
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and platform requirements

3. Aggregated and De-Identified Reports

We may use submitted content, analysis results, and security indicators to create aggregated or de-identified reports about scam trends, phishing patterns, suspicious URLs, fraud categories, and similar security topics.

These reports are designed not to identify individual users. We do not sell personal information.

4. Data Minimization and Sensitive Information

SecurityForAll is designed to reduce unnecessary collection of sensitive information where practical.

Depending on the feature used, we may attempt to redact, remove, transform, or hash certain personal information before deeper analysis. This may include phone numbers, email addresses, account-like identifiers, or similar values.

However, automated redaction and detection may not identify every sensitive item. You are responsible for avoiding the submission of passwords, payment card numbers, government IDs, medical records, private account credentials, or other highly sensitive information.

5. AI and Automated Analysis

Some SecurityForAll features may use automated systems, artificial intelligence, machine learning, threat intelligence tools, URL reputation services, or other security analysis providers to evaluate submitted content.

Automated results are informational only and may not always be accurate. SecurityForAll does not guarantee that every scam, phishing attempt, unsafe website, malware indicator, or fraudulent message will be detected.

6. How We Share Information

We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate, secure, and improve SecurityForAll. These may include:

  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Backend infrastructure providers
  • AI or automated analysis providers
  • Threat intelligence or URL reputation providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Crash reporting providers
  • Payment and subscription processing providers
  • Customer support providers
  • Security and fraud-prevention providers

These service providers may process information only as needed to provide services to us, operate the app, maintain security, process subscriptions, support users, or comply with legal obligations.

We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, government request, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the public, our services, or others.

We do not sell personal information.

7. App Permissions

SecurityForAll may request access to certain device features only when needed for app functionality.

Depending on the features you use, permissions may include:

  • Camera access, used to scan QR codes when you choose to use the QR scanner
  • Network access, used to communicate with our backend services and analyze submitted content
  • User-selected image or file access, used when you choose to upload a screenshot or image for analysis

SecurityForAll does not use these permissions to access your content without your action. We do not automatically scan your SMS messages, call logs, contacts, notifications, clipboard, or files.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Raw submitted content may be processed temporarily to provide analysis. We may retain analysis results, risk scores, security indicators, diagnostic logs, abuse-prevention records, subscription records, and support records for service operation, security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, customer support, and app improvement.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, user settings, legal requirements, operational needs, and security needs.
